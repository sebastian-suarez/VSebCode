Icons v2 — pilot

M20 · D22 style R1 “True colour” · 2026-09-03 · 24 icons · fix round after the 2026-09-03 gate

Twenty-four icons, built by the production recipe, before any mass work starts. The roster is deliberate: 10 carried file marks frozen by D22 and refitted here byte for byte, so the pilot proves the toolchain reproduces the ruling exactly; 6 new file marks picked straight out of the v1 autopsy — the freehand redraws, the drifted hues and the letter chaos (npm, dotenv, yaml, git, go, vue); and 4 folder pairs, closed and open, which is where the one genuinely new rule lives. Every mark's geometry comes from the brand's own vector artwork or simple-icons; nothing is drawn from memory. This revision is the fix round: docker and editorconfig were rejected at the gate and rebuilt — section 4 shows them — and nothing else in the set moved. Judge it at 16 px, in the tree, first.

THE ICON IS THE MARK

R1: official geometry, official colours, multi-colour kept. A file icon is byte-identical to its master; a folder is the same master knocked out white on a concept-hue body. Both are asserted, not asserted-to.

EVERY CUT IS LOGGED

14 subjects carry simplifications and each one names the measurement that forced it. Three icons needed the prettier rider — dotenv, prettier itself, and now editorconfig, whose 0.2 px linework cannot survive 16 px as line.

GATES BEFORE OPINIONS

Format, carry identity (now 10 frozen + 2 superseded by ruling), derivation identity, folder mass, provenance, letter audit, 16 px proofs, fidelity strips and the R7/R8 twin audit all run — 0 twins, 0 form collisions.

5 · Flags — every judgement call, numbered

Nothing here was decided quietly. Each item is a call that wants a verdict, or a number you should see before giving one. The 8 marked RULED — APPROVED 2026-09-03 already have one; flags 14–20 are new with the fix round and do not.

1

NEW RULE — the open-folder construction and its second toneRULED — APPROVED 2026-09-03

L7 makes closed and open ONE construction, so the open state may not pick its own colours. The pilot keeps v1's proven two-panel open silhouette verbatim (back sheet standing behind, pocket tipped forward and jutting past the right edge) and derives the second tone by formula: shade(body) = hsl(h, s, max(18, l − 15)) — hue and saturation untouched, floored so a dark brand body cannot collapse into the backdrop. One formula for every folder, sand included; it reproduces v1's own hand-picked sand pair (#BF9354 → #8F6D37; the formula gives #906B36) to within two units per channel. Consequence you should look at: the face mark is byte-identical in both states, so on the open folder it crosses the seam between the two panels — see src/ and test/ in the tree above. The panels differ only in lightness, so the white glyph reads continuously, but it is painted over both sheets rather than on the pocket alone. The alternative is a smaller or lower mark in the open state, which breaks the one-construction rule and drops folder-node under L7's 8 px.

2

npm ships its SQUARE lockup, not its wordmarkRULED — APPROVED 2026-09-03

npm publishes two marks. The wide wordmark (780×250) lands on 0.97 px stems at the widest fit the 16-grid allows. The square mark — npm square/n.svg, authored by npm at 16×16 — lands on 1.60 px and needs no reduction at all, so that is what ships, geometry untouched, hard corners and all. Two colour notes: npm's own file ships #C12127, and brand-colors.json (L2's declared colour source of truth) records npm red as #CB3837. The pilot uses #CB3837. Same shape of question for git (#f03c2e in the file, #F05032 in brand-colors) and go (#00acd7 vs #00ADD8). One rule, applied uniformly — but it is a rule that has not been ratified.

3

dotenv — the prettier rider fires, and this is the weakest icon in the pilotRULED — APPROVED 2026-09-03

Measured: the official ".ENV" bar is 0.50 px at the compact envelope, and still only 0.77 px if the square is thrown away and the bare wordmark is stretched across the whole grid. Unreadable at any allowed fit, so the rider applies. What ships: the official field, the official ink, and the official dot + E letterform scaled 2.5× as one group so the E's bar lands on 1.24 px. N and V are gone. Alternatives measured and rejected: "ENV" without the dot (0.93 px), ".EN" (0.72 px), the field alone with no glyph at all. If ".E" reads wrong to you, the honest fallbacks are the bare yellow field or a single "E".

4

dotenv's black is NOT lifted — a clarification to the visibility-lift ruleRULED — APPROVED 2026-09-03

The documented lift raises any official hex below L 22 to L 88 (markdown's #000000 is the only case in round 2). dotenv's ink is #000000 too, but it prints on the mark's own yellow field and never meets the backdrop. Lifting it would invent a colour the brand does not use. The pilot therefore reads the rule as applying to ink that MEETS THE BACKDROP. New wording, needs ratifying.

5

yaml owns a mark after all — and it is a hard read at 16 pxRULED — APPROVED 2026-09-03

The brief expected yaml to be mark-less. It is not: yaml.org ships the stacked YA / ML lockup and simple-icons carries a faithful vector of it, so the neutral vocabulary does not apply and the icon is the real mark in the real red. Honest verdict on the 16 px proof: it reads as a red letter BLOCK — the four letters are present and their bars clear the 1.2 px official floor (1.25 px after the envelope was widened to 13.6), but they are not individually legible at 16 px. That is the mark's own construction, not a fit failure. Reducing to the top row ("YA") would double the stems and stop being the mark.

6

Two envelopes widened, both forced by L5

git 12.8 → 13.6 (official branch connector 1.14 → 1.21 px) and yaml 12.8 → 13.6 (thinnest bar 1.17 → 1.25 px). 13.6 is not a new number — it is react's envelope, and all three marks share the same reason for it: their corners are empty, so the bounding box may be larger without the icon reading larger. Recorded as ENV.open in the manifest.

7

go loses its motion lines

The three speed lines are 3.7/78.4 of the mark — 0.37 px at any allowed fit. Dropped under L5, which is also what lets the "GO" itself fill the flat envelope and land on a 2.09 px ring wall. The letterforms are the brand's own outlines, not typeset letters.

8

vue is two-tone, and the dark half measures 2.01:1 against the backdropRULED — APPROVED 2026-09-03

The two layers come from vuejs/art's own logo.svg — no derivation games, no freehand. The inner V is #35495E, and against the #121314 editor ground that is 2.01:1. It does NOT trip the documented visibility lift (that rule fires below L 22; #35495E sits at L 28.8), and applying the lift anyway would turn Vue's dark half into a pale grey and destroy the mark. So the pilot ships it faithful and puts the number in front of you. Mitigation in the geometry: the dark V is enclosed by green on every side except a 0.39 px rim at the top. Precedent worth knowing: eslint's official outer purple #4B32C3 measures 2.25:1 on the same backdrop and D22 already ruled it in, so this is a band the set has accepted once — but it was never stated as a rule.

9

folder-test uses the check, not the beakerRULED — APPROVED 2026-09-03

Mark-less concept, so the glyph comes from the neutral vocabulary. The check is ONE sub-shape with a constant 2.9-unit stem and lands 9.78 px of ink on the face; a beaker needs a neck and a body, and the neck is about 1.1 px at that scale. It also keeps v1's association, so nobody has to relearn the row.

10

folder-docker drops the containersRULED — APPROVED 2026-09-03

L7 asks the folder glyph to be the file icon's mark simplified. The container grid holds 1.21 px gaps on the file icon but only 0.85 px once the mark is squeezed onto the 10.2 px face, so the whale body carries the face alone — official subpath, untouched, one sub-shape inside L7's two-shape budget. Unchanged by the fix round: the face master and both folder icons are byte-identical to the build you approved; only the FILE icon's deck moved.

11

Twin audit: 0 twins, 0 form collisions, 7 colour hits separated by form

The four blues behave: only typescript ↔ docker lands inside R7's thresholds (dh 5.4 / dL 5.1 / dS 24.5) and their forms score 0.039, so they are a hue neighbourhood, not a twin. docker ↔ react (dh 13.1) and docker ↔ go (dh 14.0) are outside the thresholds. Deliberately tolerated, all separated by form: rust↔npm 0.100, rust↔yaml 0.321, rust↔git 0.298, npm↔yaml 0.078, python↔dotenv 0.124, and folder-src↔folder-test — identical sand by law, separated only by the glyph at 0.212. Re-run after the fix round: editorconfig's dominant fill is still its near-white face (#FDFDFD, S 0, neutral lane) and its new #020202 ink is bucketed with it, so markdown ↔ editorconfig stays a neutral-lane pair separated by form (0.131, was 0.201).

12

New sub-rule in the twin audit: the PLATE lane

typescript, npm and dotenv are all an official rectangle carrying a glyph. Scored on their silhouettes they collide at 0.997–1.000, which says nothing — the rectangle is the brands' choice, not ours. The audit therefore ports m11's BADGE treatment: two plates are compared GLYPH to glyph, against the higher 0.92 bar. They score 0.129–0.297. The plate flag is a property of the artwork, declared per subject, not a judgement made inside the audit.

13

Three carried icons are over the 2 KB advisory target

rust 3654 B, editorconfig 2640 B, python 2168 B — all well under the 4 KB hard cap and all three the price of official geometry (L8's round-2 erratum). editorconfig came DOWN from 3700 B in the fix round even though it now carries the whole drawing: painting the ink contour once and the official counters back on top costs less than emitting the contour twice.

14

FIX ROUND · editorconfig is re-sourced from EditorConfig's own vector

The rejected version came from simple-icons. Checking the brand first, as L2 asks: editorconfig/editorconfig holds assets/EditorConfig_Logo.svg, a CorelDRAW export of the mascot — so the brand DOES publish a vector, and it is now the source. That changes provenance and palette together: the face is the file's own #FDFDFD (was a #FEFEFE sampled off logo.png), the ear interiors are #FDF2F2 and #FEF3F3, the ink is #020202. The file is kept in sources-svg/editorconfig-official.svg for the standing fidelity gate. The call: a brand asset found in a code repo rather than a press kit is still the brand's asset, and it outranks simple-icons under L2's preference order. Worth ratifying. Licence, honestly: that repo ships no LICENSE file and GitHub reports license: null, so unlike every other source in this pilot the file carries no declared terms — it is a brand mark used for the brand's own file type, which is what every icon theme does, but it is not CC0 like the source it replaced. The fallback was measured, not assumed: simple-icons' trace has the SAME subpath structure and the identical construction on it renders the same icon — but at 4475 B against this file's 2640 B, which is over L8's 4 KB hard cap, so going back to CC0 geometry would also need a further reduction to pass the format gate. Your call, and it is a real trade rather than a free one.

15

FIX ROUND · how the mascot survives 16 px: solid features, not lines

Measured at the shipped fit: the head contour is 0.33 px of ink and the spectacle rims are 0.16–0.21 px. No fit makes a 0.2 px rim readable, so the prettier rider applies and the reduction is done by NOT painting three official counters back: the lens interiors and pupils stay ink, which merges rim + lens + pupil into one solid lens each (3.45×1.91 px left, 3.61×1.97 px right, with the official 0.56 px bridge gap still open between them); and the nostril stays ink, which turns a 0.2 px outline into a solid 1.82×0.89 px nose. Two things to know. The nose's minor axis is 0.89 px, under the 1.2 px floor — kept because the only alternative is the 0.2 px ring. And the head contour, ear rims and whisker are still in the file at 0.16–0.33 px: they render as antialiasing, which is what gives the icon its drawn feel at 32 and 64 px, and they cost nothing at 16.

16

FIX ROUND · editorconfig's ink is #020202 and is NOT lifted — with one honest edge

Flag 4's clarification says the visibility lift applies to ink that meets the BACKDROP, not to ink printed on the mark's own field. editorconfig's ink prints on the white face, so it is not lifted — lifting it to L 88 would erase the mascot's glasses. The edge: that same ink also forms the drawing's outer contour, so a 0.33 px rim of #020202 does sit against #121314, at 1.12:1. It reads as a soft edge on the white face rather than as a line, and the visible silhouette is the face itself — but the number is in the manifest and you should see it.

17

FIX ROUND · the pink ear interiors are kept and are invisible at 16 px

The official ears are #FDF2F2 and #FEF3F3 against a #FDFDFD face — about one unit of lightness apart. They are real official colours and the achromatic exemption (L6 erratum 2) protects them from any saturation clamp, so the pilot ships them; at 16 px they are indistinguishable from the face and only the ear rims separate the ears. Two 100-byte paths that do nothing at the target size. The alternative is dropping them and shipping a one-colour face, which is smaller and 16-px-identical but stops being the brand's palette. Kept faithful; say the word and they go.

18

FIX ROUND · docker's deck: three columns is the ceiling, and 3+3+1 fills it

The whale's back is flat from x 0.6 to 17.4 and its tail fin stands at x 17.79, which is why the official deck really spans x 2.03–16.29 (14.26 u, keeping 1.50 u of fin clearance). FOUR official-size boxes need 15.65 u to hold the 1.2 px floor. Pushed in anyway, the fin clearance drops to 0.38–0.40 px and the last container fuses with the tail; squeezed inside the deck instead, the box shrinks to 83% of official at a 1.20 px gap. Both are rejected. Three columns fit comfortably, so the cargo is restored by FILLING them: 3+3+1, seven of the official nine, box untouched, every gap 1.21 px, fin clearance 1.74 px. The top box sits on the right-hand column because that is where the official mark puts its top box — at the right end of the tier below. Every layout measured is rendered side by side in pilot/proofs/docker-deck-candidates.png, including the legal ones that carry less cargo (3+2 at five, 3+3 and 3+2+1 at six) — if seven reads busy to you, six is one line of code away.

19

FIX ROUND · docker's optical mass moved 140 → 151

The third tier makes the mark taller than the wide envelope, so the fit becomes height-bound: the ink is 14.50×10.40 instead of 15.20×9.18 and the whale is 4.6% smaller. That is inside the set's range (go 118, git 185) and the envelope constant itself did not move, but it is a real change to a carried subject's mass and it is here rather than buried in the manifest.

20

FIX ROUND · the D22 carry gate is amended, not switched off

The pilot's carry gate asserted that all twelve D22-carried subjects still refit to samples/masters/ byte for byte. Two of them were rejected at this gate, so that assertion is now two-sided: 10 of 12 byte-identical, and exactly docker and editorconfig SUPERSEDED BY PILOT (2026-09-03). A superseded subject that quietly refits to its round-2 bytes fails the gate just as loudly as a frozen one that drifted, so the amendment cannot hide a regression. The ruling date is recorded in the gate output and in manifest.json.
