# M11 icon-concept inventory — statistics

Merged survey of the three most-installed VS Code file-icon themes, deduplicated into one
concept list. Generated by `1-extract.js` → `2-merge.js` → `3-core.js`; raw upstream files
are kept under `sources/` as provenance.

Read-outs worth noting:

- The **shared core is small**: only 189 of 1534 concepts (12%) exist in all three themes,
  and 533 (35%) in two or more. Everything else is one theme's long tail.
- **Material owns folders** (284 concepts vs vscode-icons' 179 and Great Icons' 21); the
  Material+vscode-icons pair accounts for 84 of the 88 two-source folder concepts.
- **vscode-icons owns file granularity** — it splits where the others lump (`cheader` vs
  `c`, `scss` vs `sass`, `plsql-package-body` vs `plsql`), which is why it carries 571
  unique concepts. That granularity is why the brief prefers its naming.
- **Great Icons is almost a strict subset**: 235 of its 261 concepts (90%) are covered by at
  least one bigger theme. It is useful as a *tiebreaker on what is actually common*, not as
  a source of new concepts.

## Naming decisions

Canonical ids are kebab-case and follow **vscode-icons** naming when the sources disagree
(`js` not `javascript`, `reactjs`/`reactts` not `react`/`react_ts`, `shell` not `console`,
`cheader`/`cppheader` not `h`/`hpp`, `dartlang` not `dart`, `text` not `document`,
`dotenv` not `tune`, `cert` not `certificate`).

Eleven deliberate deviations, kept in `OVERRIDE_NAMING` in `2-merge.js`:
`makefile` (vscode-icons calls it `gnu`), `pascal` (it calls it `delphi`), and the plural
forms Sebastian actually uses — `scripts`, `components`, `styles`, `assets`, `utils`,
`types`, `hooks`, `i18n` (it calls it `locale`), `turborepo` (it calls it `turbo`).

## Known data caveats

- **Matcher conflicts are real, not parse errors.** The merged `match` arrays are the *union*
  of three themes that disagree, so a matcher can legitimately appear under two concepts
  (Great Icons maps the `typescriptreact` language id to its plain TypeScript icon; Material
  lumps `.csv`/`.tsv` under its spreadsheet icon while vscode-icons puts them under `text`).
  54 such conflicts fall inside the core tier; all 54 are listed with a resolved winner in
  `core-tier.json → matcherCollisions`, 33 of them hand-pinned because the default
  "lowest rank wins" rule would have picked the broader concept.
- **10 concepts have no matchers at all** (`aws`, `compass`, `elasticbeanstalk`, `go-gopher`,
  `meteor`, `php-elephant`, `php-elephant-pink`, `rails`, `style`, `view`). These are
  alternate artwork or folder-only decorations in their source; they are kept for
  completeness but cannot be wired to anything.
- **Material's `patterns:` DSL is expanded, not dropped.** `mapPatterns` from
  `src/core/patterns/patterns.ts` is reimplemented in `1-extract.js`, so `Cosmiconfig`,
  `Ecmascript`, `NodeEcosystem`, `Configuration`, `Yaml` and `Dotfile` patterns produce the
  same filenames the extension ships (this is where most of Material's ~2000 `.*rc.*`
  filenames come from).
- **vscode-icons' `filenamesGlob` × `extensionsGlob`** is expanded as a cartesian product
  (`['astro.config'] × ['js','ts'] → astro.config.js, astro.config.ts`). Missing this on the
  first pass left 41 concepts matcher-less.
- **Disabled vscode-icons entries are excluded.** 131 entries carry `disabled: true` (123 file
  + 8 folder — the
  `access2`/`babel2`/`ai2` alternate artwork); including them would have inflated its count
  and created phantom duplicate concepts.
