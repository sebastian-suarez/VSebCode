# VSCodium comparison exposes remaining fork gaps

**The fork did not reproduce all VSCodium behavior.** The most relevant remaining work is the transitive proprietary signer in the build dependency tree, Microsoft branding in policy output, missing generic extension-compatibility product data, and three dead Command Palette settings left after chat removal. The first two are concrete build/branding gaps; the compatibility data needs selective decisions and packaged extension tests, while the settings are unused cleanup. The fork already removes more of the AI, telemetry, account, tunnel and cloud stack than VSCodium’s corresponding defaults and UI guards. Other differences add optional features or support deferred platforms, and several VSCodium patches are inactive, obsolete or target products deliberately removed here. The complete ledger below covers **all 76 tracked patch-related files**, plus the non-patch preparation and distribution behavior that a patch-only comparison would miss. ## Deferred platforms and intentional removals narrow the actual backlog

**Linux gaps are recorded, with validation deferred.** A surviving policy service still reads `/etc/vscode/policy.json`; Flatpak socket placement, XDG state-directory logs, shell-interpreted external-terminal commands, RPM preservation of edited product metadata and Debian `libasound2t64` alternatives are absent. Generic alternate Electron/sysroot acquisition and PPC64LE, RISC-V, Loong64 and s390x source/task support are also absent. These require a chosen platform/distribution scope, not a blanket declaration that every VSCodium lane ships or works: VSCodium’s standard s390x lane is REH only, Loong64’s patch omits some package guards, and its PPC runtime is Electron 41.0.3 rather than the fork’s 43.7.7. **Windows gaps are likewise deferred.** SDK signtool discovery is absent, although strip-before-resource-edit and fork metadata already exist.
